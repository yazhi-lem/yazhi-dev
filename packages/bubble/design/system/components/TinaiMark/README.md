# TinaiMark

One flat mark per tiṇai, drawn in `currentColor` on a 24-unit grid: a sun over a dune (Palai), the jasmine (Mullai), the ridge and its bloom (Kurinji), a paddy stalk over its rows (Marutham), a wave under the moon (Neytal).

**The consumer provides** `tinai`, `size` (px, default 20), `className`. Solid shapes only: they read at 14px and sit inside a bubble. Decorative (`aria-hidden`) — the button around it carries the name.

Source: `packages/bubble/src/tinai/TinaiMark.tsx`.
