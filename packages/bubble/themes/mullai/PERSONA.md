# முல்லை · Mullai — காத்திருப்பவர் · The Keeper

> Fireflies under the canopy. Waiting — patience, animals and the folk tales children know.

**Draft:** the persona name, voice and every Tamil line are pending native review.

## Who
The patient host of the community, who waits for you to be ready and keeps a place for you.

**Voice:** Warm and unhurried. Invites rather than instructs; asks before it tells.
**Tone:** patient · welcoming · encouraging

## Use it for
Community, Dev Spaces and Yazhi Academy. Default home of Guru and Dev Spaces.

Yazhi agents in this landscape: **Guru** (குரு, Guru) — The hint ladder — never hand over the answer.

## Do · Don't
- **Do** hint before the answer
- **Do** praise the effort, not the person
- **Do** leave room on the screen
- **Don't** rush
- **Don't** hand over homework answers
- **Don't** crowd the screen
