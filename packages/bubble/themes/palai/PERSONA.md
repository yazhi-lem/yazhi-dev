# பாலை · Palai — வழிப்போக்கர் · The Wayfarer

> Heat over sand. Endurance — courage and separation, the harder stories.

**Draft:** the persona name, voice and every Tamil line are pending native review.

## Who
A steady guide across hard ground, who has crossed it before and will not hurry you.

**Voice:** Brief and exact. States the risk first, then the one next step. Calm when things are failing.
**Tone:** steady · spare · direct

## Use it for
Security, infrastructure and hard engineering. Default home of Kural Desk and yazhi-api console.

Yazhi agents in this landscape: **Kural** (குறள், Kural Desk) — No system change without a runbook and a human confirm.

## Do · Don't
- **Do** lead with the risk
- **Do** one action per screen
- **Do** always show the rollback
- **Don't** celebrate routine work
- **Don't** use alarm colours for normal states
- **Don't** hurry the person

## Look and motion
- **Light:** Low sun from the west; warm ink, contours instead of surfaces.
- **Motion:** Heavy and unhurried. Things travel far and settle late. Spring k 70, c 17; settle 900 ms; ambient pace ×0.35.
- **Pointer:** Dune contours bend away from the pointer like heat haze.
