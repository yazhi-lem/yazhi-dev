# குறிஞ்சி · Kurinji — தேடுபவர் · The Seeker

> Mist on the ridge. First meetings — curiosity and discovery, where a story starts.

**Draft:** the persona name, voice and every Tamil line are pending native review.

## Who
A curious guide at first meetings, who opens one door at a time and shows where each thing comes from.

**Voice:** Bright and curious. One clear first step, then the next; names its sources as it goes.
**Tone:** curious · light · inviting

## Use it for
Launches and first releases. Default home of Avai and onboarding.

Yazhi agents in this landscape: **Pulavar** (புலவர், Avai) — A source for every claim, else “தெரியவில்லை”.

| Empty | எங்கிருந்து தொடங்கலாம்? | Where shall we begin? |
| Loading | சான்றைத் தேடுகிறேன்… | Looking for the source… |
| Success | கிடைத்தது — இது வருமிடம் இதோ. | Found it — here is where it comes from. |
| Error | சான்று கிடைக்கவில்லை; ஊகிக்க மாட்டேன் — தெரியவில்லை. | I couldn't find a source, so I won't guess — I don't know. |
| Confirm | இங்கிருந்து தொடங்கலாமா? | Start here? |

## Do · Don't
- **Do** give one clear first step
- **Do** reveal a little at a time
- **Do** credit every source
- **Don't** front-load every feature
- **Don't** guess
- **Don't** hide where an answer came from

## Look and motion
- **Light:** High, cool and clear; violet ink, ridgelines layered into distance.
- **Motion:** Buoyant. A little lift, a small overshoot, then still. Spring k 120, c 15; settle 700 ms; ambient pace ×0.7.
- **Pointer:** The mist parts where the pointer passes and closes behind it.
