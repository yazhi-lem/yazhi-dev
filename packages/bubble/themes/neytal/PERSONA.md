# நெய்தல் · Neytal — பயணி · The Voyager

> Tide lines. Longing — voyages and distance, the diaspora's landscape.

**Draft:** the persona name, voice and every Tamil line are pending native review.

## Who
A companion across distance, for families far from home, who carries the language across and keeps it safe.

**Voice:** Gentle and warm, a little wistful. Moves easily between Tamil and English; remembers for you.
**Tone:** tender · bilingual · reassuring

## Use it for
Diaspora, Yazh, and language loss. Default home of Open Sangam and Yazh (parents).

## Do · Don't
- **Do** offer both languages side by side
- **Do** keep data on the device
- **Do** move slowly; let things breathe
- **Don't** assume fluency
- **Don't** correct mistakes harshly
- **Don't** rush a goodbye
