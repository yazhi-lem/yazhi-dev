# App icons

Store and launcher icons for Guru, Nyaya and Kural: the app's Tamil name in Baloo Thambi 2 SemiBold, ink `tn-on-pastel` (#1b1b1b) on its thiṇai pastel, full bleed.

| App | Pastel | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Guru · குரு | `tn-mullai` #b5e2bd | `guru-app-icon-1024.png`, `guru-app-icon-512.png`, `guru-app-icon.svg` |
| Nyaya · நியாயா | `tn-marutham` #f4dc86 | `nyaya-app-icon-1024.png`, `nyaya-app-icon-512.png`, `nyaya-app-icon.svg` |
| Kural · குரல் | `tn-paalai` #f6c2a2 | `kural-app-icon-1024.png`, `kural-app-icon-512.png`, `kural-app-icon.svg` |

- **Upload the PNGs.** 1024 × 1024 is the App Store and master size; 512 × 512 is Google Play's. They are square and full bleed: the stores round the corners.
- The SVGs are the editable source. Their text needs Baloo Thambi 2 installed; outline it before using the SVG anywhere else.
- The full Tamil name is used, not the first letter, because Guru and Kural both begin with கு.
- The Tamil names carry † until a Pulavar signs them off. If a name changes, regenerate all three sizes.
