# Code of Conduct

Yazhi is built in the open by people from many places, languages and backgrounds. This code of conduct applies to
everyone who takes part: in our repositories, on circle.yazhi.dev, at Dev Space sessions and build nights, and anywhere
you represent the Yazhi community.

**You must accept this code before you join the community.**

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Yazhi to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, language, dialect, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We will act and talk in ways that keep this community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second or third language.
- Respect every language and dialect. Nobody's Tamil (or Telugu, or English) is "wrong" because it is regional,
  colloquial or classical.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person. Say what would make a change better.
- Credit people. When you build on someone's idea, code, data or translation, name them.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Look after the shared space: keep threads on topic and help others find answers.
- Protect other people's data. Never post someone's personal details, private messages or data without their consent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted contact or attention.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about any of the characteristics listed in the pledge, including caste, religion,
  region, language or dialect.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussion, or sustained disruption of sessions or threads.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing), such as a phone number, address or email, without explicit permission.
- Sharing anyone's personal data from datasets, logs or screenshots.
- Plagiarism: passing off someone else's code, writing, translation or data as your own.
- Encouraging or threatening any of the above.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, report it. You do not have to be the person affected.

- **Email:** `[CONDUCT CONTACT EMAIL — to be set before this file is published]`
- **On circle.yazhi.dev:** use the flag button on a post, or message the moderators group.
- **At an in-person or online session:** tell the session host or any moderator.

Include what happened, when and where, and links or screenshots if you have them. If you are not sure whether something
counts, report it anyway.

## What happens after a report

1. **Acknowledge.** A moderator on the current rota replies within **24 hours**.
2. **Review.** Moderators look at what happened. They talk to the people involved where it is safe and useful. Anyone with
   a conflict of interest (for example, the person reported, or a close collaborator) steps out of the review.
3. **Decide.** Moderators choose an outcome from the ladder below and tell the reporter what was decided, without sharing
   other people's private details.
4. **Record.** Every report and outcome is logged privately, so repeated behaviour is visible over time.

Reports are handled confidentially. We do not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Enforcement ladder

| Step | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | A one-off lapse, such as unprofessional language | A private note from a moderator explaining what went wrong. A public apology may be asked for |
| 2. Warning | A more serious incident, or repeated lapses | A warning with consequences. No contact with the people involved for a set time |
| 3. Temporary ban | Serious or sustained breaches | No interaction in any community space for a set period |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of breaches, harassment, or aggression towards a person or group | Permanent removal from all community spaces |

Moderators may skip steps when the harm is serious.

## Who handles reports

A rota of community moderators, with a named backup for every slot, handles reports. The current moderators are listed
on circle.yazhi.dev. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face action from the rest of the
moderation team.
