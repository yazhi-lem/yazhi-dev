## 1. Executive Summary & Context

`yazhi-dev` serves as the public-facing developer hub, portal, and community entrypoint for the Yazhi Sovereign Tamil AI ecosystem. It aggregates resources, onboarding documentation, API client quickstarts, and links across all core repositories (`adhan`, `open-sangam`, `yazhi-api`, `illakiya`, `yazh-unity`, `yazhi-skills`).
